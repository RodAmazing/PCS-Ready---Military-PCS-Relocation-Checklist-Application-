The idea behind PCS Ready and the military families it is designed to support.

About the Creator

My name is Rodney Ragin, and I am a Chief Warrant Officer Two (CW2) in the U.S. Army with 15 years of service.

My wife is also an Army Warrant Officer, and together we have three children: one son and two daughters.

Being a dual-military family, we understand the challenges of balancing military responsibilities, family life, and frequent moves.

Why PCS Ready?

My family recently PCS'd to Hawaii, and the experience reminded me how stressful moving can be.

I created PCS Ready to help military families stay organized by keeping their checklists, important documents, and reminders in one place.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make military moves less stressful through clear steps, accessible information, and timely reminders.

PCS Ready is designed to provide military families with an organized and convenient way to prepare for their next duty station.
