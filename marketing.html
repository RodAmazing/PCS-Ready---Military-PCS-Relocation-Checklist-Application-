Move With a Plan, Not the Stress

PCS Ready is designed to help service members and their families stay organized throughout the Permanent Change of Station process.

From receiving orders to arriving at your next duty station, PCS Ready helps keep important tasks and deadlines in one place.

Who Is PCS Ready For?

PCS Ready is designed for active-duty service members, military spouses, and families preparing to relocate to their next duty station.

Whether you are completing your first PCS move or have relocated multiple times, PCS Ready is intended to provide a simple and organized moving experience.

Our Marketing Strategy

PCS Ready would be promoted through military-focused social media communities, installation relocation resources, and informational demonstrations.

The marketing campaign would highlight the application's convenience, organization tools, and potential to reduce relocation-related stress.

Future outreach could include partnerships with military family support organizations and relocation assistance programs.

Why Choose PCS Ready?

Hardware Requirements

PCS Ready was developed using a MacBook Air computer. The application prototype does not require expensive equipment or specialized hardware to operate.

Users can access the published website through a desktop computer, laptop, tablet, or smartphone with a modern web browser and an internet connection.

Software Requirements

PCS Ready was developed using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. HTML provides the structure of the website, while CSS controls the appearance, colors, and layout.

JavaScript provides the interactive features, including the PCS checklist, progress tracker, and reset button.

Development Tools

TextEdit was used to write and edit the website code on macOS. Safari was used to preview and test the application prototype.

System Compatibility

PCS Ready is designed to work with modern web browsers such as Safari, Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Mozilla Firefox.

Why Choose PCS Ready?

Military families already have enough to manage during a PCS move. Traditional paper checklists can get lost, and keeping track of tasks across multiple websites can become frustrating.

PCS Ready brings important moving tasks together in one convenient location. Users can track their progress, check off completed requirements, and add personalized tasks based on their move.

The goal is simple: spend less time worrying about what needs to get done and more time preparing for the next duty station.
