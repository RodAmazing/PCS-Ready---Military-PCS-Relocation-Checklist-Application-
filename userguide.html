PCS Ready User Guide

Welcome to PCS Ready! This guide explains how to use the interactive PCS checklist and manage your relocation tasks.

Follow the instructions below to get started and explore the features available in the application prototype.

Getting Started

Step 1: Open PCS Ready

Open the PCS Ready website using your preferred web browser.

Step 2: Access the Checklist

Select Application Info from the navigation menu to open the interactive PCS checklist.

Step 3: Review Your Tasks

Review the seven PCS tasks provided and identify which requirements you have already completed.

Marking Tasks Complete

When you complete a relocation task, click the checkbox beside it. A checkmark will appear to show that the task has been completed.

If you select a task by mistake, click the checkbox again to remove the checkmark.

Tracking Your Progress

The progress tracker automatically calculates the percentage of tasks you have completed.

As you check off tasks, the progress bar increases. If you uncheck a task, the percentage decreases to reflect your current progress.

Adding a Custom PCS Task

PCS Ready allows you to add tasks that are specific to your move.

  1. Open the Application Info page.
  2. Find the Add Your Own PCS Task section.
  3. Type your task into the text box.
  4. Click the Add Task button.
  5. Your new task will appear in the checklist.
  6. Check the box when the task is completed.

For example, you could add tasks such as contacting your sponsor, scheduling a housing inspection, or registering children for school.

Custom tasks are included in your overall progress percentage.

Resetting Your Checklist

If you want to start tracking your progress again, click the Reset Checklist button.

This button removes all checkmarks and returns the progress tracker to 0% Complete.

Any custom tasks you added will remain on the checklist, but their checkmarks will also be cleared.

Saving Your Progress

PCS Ready is currently a prototype and does not permanently save your checklist progress.

Refreshing or reopening the page will return the checklist to its original seven tasks and remove any custom tasks you added.

Need to Start Over?

To clear your completed tasks while keeping your custom tasks, use the Reset Checklist button.

To return to the original seven PCS tasks, refresh the page.

Ready to Get Started?

Now that you understand how to use PCS Ready, you can begin organizing your next military move.
