PCS Ready User Guide

Welcome to PCS Ready! This guide will walk you through the basic features of the application prototype and explain how to use the interactive PCS checklist.

Our goal is to make the PCS process easier to understand and help military families stay organized throughout their relocation.

Getting Started With PCS Ready

Step 1: Open PCS Ready

Open the PCS Ready website and review the homepage to learn about the application's purpose and available features.

Step 2: Access Your Checklist

Click Application Info in the navigation menu to access the interactive PCS checklist.

Step 3: Review Your Tasks

Review the checklist for important relocation tasks such as PCS orders, household goods shipments, housing, travel, and installation clearing.

How to Use the PCS Checklist

Marking Tasks Complete

When you complete a PCS task, click the checkbox beside that task. A checkmark will appear to indicate that the task has been completed.

Tracking Your Progress

As you complete tasks, the progress bar automatically updates to show the percentage of checklist items completed.

Resetting Your Checklist

To start over, click the Reset Checklist button. This will clear all selected tasks and return your progress to zero percent.

Understanding the Prototype

PCS Ready is currently a website-based application prototype developed for the ITCC298 Final Capstone Project.

The interactive checklist and progress tracker demonstrate how the application could help military families organize their relocation tasks.

Checklist selections are not permanently saved. Refreshing the page will reset the checklist to its original state.

Future Features

Future versions of PCS Ready could include personalized PCS timelines, automatic deadline reminders, secure document storage, and user accounts that save checklist progress.

How to Add a Custom PCS Task

PCS Ready allows users to create their own tasks because every military move is different. Follow these steps to add a personalized task.

  1. Open the Application Info page.
  2. Scroll down to the interactive PCS checklist.
  3. Find the Add Your Own PCS Task section.
  4. Type a task into the text box.
  5. Click the Add Task button.
  6. Your new task will appear in the checklist.
  7. Check the box when the task is completed.
  8. The progress tracker will update automatically.

For example, users can add tasks such as scheduling a housing inspection, contacting their sponsor, or registering children for school.
