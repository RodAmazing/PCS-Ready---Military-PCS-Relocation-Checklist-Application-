About the PCS Ready Application

PCS Ready is a website-based application prototype designed to help military service members and their families stay organized during a Permanent Change of Station (PCS) move.

The application brings common relocation tasks together in one checklist, allowing users to monitor their progress and add tasks based on their individual moving requirements.

Application Features

Interactive Checklist

Review important PCS requirements, including orders, household goods shipments, vehicle transportation, housing, and installation clearing. Mark tasks complete as you finish them.

Progress Tracking

Monitor your relocation preparation with a progress tracker that automatically calculates the percentage of completed checklist tasks.

Custom PCS Tasks

Add personalized tasks to your checklist based on your family's specific needs and relocation responsibilities.

Application Requirements

Compatible Devices

PCS Ready can be accessed using a desktop computer, laptop, tablet, or smartphone. No specialized equipment is required.

Internet and Browser Access

Users need an internet connection and a modern web browser, such as Safari, Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Firefox. No additional software installation is required to use the website.

Current Prototype Limitations

PCS Ready is currently a demonstration application. Checklist progress and custom tasks are not permanently saved. Refreshing the page returns the checklist to its original seven tasks.

Future Application Development

Future versions of PCS Ready could include automatic deadline reminders, secure document storage, personalized relocation timelines, and user accounts that save checklist progress. These features are planned improvements and are not available in the current prototype.
